## Top 3 recommendations

1. **Give a stuck player one free hint per level, even when the hint bank is empty.**
   Evidence: on level 14 the novice pressed Hint 24 times; with 3 max and a 2-minute recharge (`HINT_MAX`, `HINT_MS` in index.html), most presses only showed "Next free hint in m:ss". It had already used its hints on levels 6, 8, 9, 10 and 13. Without ads (itch.io build) there is no other way out. Suggestion: once moves ≥ 3× par or after a second restart on the same level, the next Hint press is free ("This one's on the house").
   Measure: novice clears ≥ 18 of 20; no level takes the bot > 60 s.
2. **Tone down the Guards danger hatching: show it only on squares you can move to right now.**
   Evidence: level 11 screenshot (both devices); about 40% of the board is red-hatched, and protected vs unprotected guards are hard to read at a glance. This is the first Guards level and it's also where the novice's restarts climb (levels 12–14: 7 restarts).
   Measure: next run's level 11 screenshot shows the checkerboard clearly; novice restarts on levels 11–16 drop below 4.
3. **Smooth the Portals finale: level 27 → 28 jumps par 8 → 12.**
   Evidence: `design.spikes` = [{27 → 28, par 8 → 12}], the only spike in the campaign; level 28 then drops to par 7 at level 29 (endless). Either add a par-10 step before it or trim level 28 to par 10.
   Measure: `design.spikes` is empty next run.
